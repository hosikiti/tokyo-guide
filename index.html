Kei's
Tokyo

Seven places and six foods I love, from my years living in the city.

SCROLL ↓
01 / TOKYO

Shibuya

渋谷

The famous crossing, neon streets and a lot of energy. I worked here for 10 years.

SWIPE →

Shibuya Crossing

Watch from the Tsutaya / Starbucks window above the crossing. Go on a weekday for a window seat.

Shibuya Tsutaya

Books and coffee next to the crossing.

Center Gai

Busy street full of shops and food, best at night.

02 / TOKYO

Roppongi

六本木

Upscale nightlife and dining. International and busy after dark.

SWIPE →

Roppongi Hills

Shopping, Mori Art Museum and a city view from the tower.

Roppongi at night

Bars and clubs. Check prices before you sit down. Last trains end around midnight.

03 / TOKYO

Harajuku

原宿

A quiet forest shrine on one side, youth pop culture on the other.

SWIPE →

Meiji Jingu

Large forest shrine. Free to enter. 10 minutes from Harajuku Station.

Takeshita Street

Crepes, fashion and character shops. Go in the morning, weekends are very crowded.

04 / TOKYO

Asakusa

浅草

Old Tokyo. A famous temple, a market street and the Skytree next door.

SWIPE →

Senso-ji

Tokyo's oldest temple. Free. Quiet before 9am.

Tokyo Skytree

About 20 min on foot from Senso-ji, or one stop by train. Go near sunset.

05 / TOKYO

Kichijoji

吉祥寺

My neighbourhood in Tokyo. Small alleys, izakaya and a big park.

SWIPE →

Harmonica Yokocho

Narrow alleys with many small bars and shops.

Inokashira Park

Walk or rent a boat. Good after lunch.

06 / DAY TRIP

Hakone

箱根

Mountains, lake and Mt Fuji views. Onsen ryokan escape.

SWIPE →

Onsen ryokan

Sleep in a tatami room, soak in the hot spring and eat dinner served in your room. This is the main reason to stay a night in Hakone.

Hakone-Yumoto

Gateway town. Shinjuku → Hakone-Yumoto by Odakyu Romancecar, about 85 min.

Lake Ashi

Pirate-ship boat and Fuji views on clear days.

07 / DAY TRIP

Kamakura

鎌倉

Old temples, the Great Buddha and the sea. Easy day trip from Tokyo.

SWIPE →

Kotoku-in (Great Buddha)

The big bronze Buddha, about 13 m tall. You can walk around it.

Hase-dera

Hillside temple with a garden and a sea view.

Enoden

Small old tram line along the coast. Ride it between Kamakura and Enoshima.

Kamakura Station

From Tokyo Station by JR, about 1 hour.

Hakone or
Kamakura?

Hakone

From Tokyo
Shinjuku → Hakone-Yumoto by Odakyu Romancecar, about 85 min
Feel
Mountains, lake and onsen towns. Stay at least one night.
Getting around
Mountain train, cable car and bus. A Hakone Free Pass can save money.
Best for
Onsen ryokan, slow trip, Fuji views

Kamakura

From Tokyo
Tokyo Station → Kamakura by JR, about 1 hour
Feel
Temples, old town and the beach. Easy in one day.
Getting around
Walk, plus the Enoden tram.
Best for
Day trip, temples, sea

Check fares and timetables before you go. Book a ryokan early for weekends.
